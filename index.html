Why preserve rare words?

Vocabulary carries traces of daily life, social relationships, objects, places, art, technology, belief, and ways of seeing the world. A digital archive allows researchers and future generations to encounter words that may no longer be common in everyday speech.

The lexical entries remain faithful to the supplied source data. Where archival information is unavailable, the site does not invent it.

ABOUT

An academic archive with a human purpose.

Project principle

This Digital Humanities project documents lesser-used Indian vocabulary in a structured, searchable form. The original lexical information is kept distinct from archival interpretation so that future researchers can extend the collection without losing sight of the source.
